OneCard Lab

LAB · not real money

A virtual school card system you can try before any hardware exists: virtual cards, canteen readers, water machines and a top-up kiosk talk to the platform through a real MQTT broker.

/lab/

Lab console

Tap cards on machines, pull network cables, carry the admin card, inject faults and watch every message.

 /admin/

School office

Students and cards, lost cards, machines, prices, top-ups, the books and reconciliation.

 /parent/

Parent app

Phone-first web app: link a child with an invitation code, see the balance and top up.

Watch the device messages

Connect any MQTT tool to the lab broker with the read-only login (default viewer / viewer; the terminal that started the lab prints the exact details).

mosquitto_sub -h 127.0.0.1 -p 1883 -u viewer -P viewer -t 'lab/v1/#' -v

Everything here is fictional: schools, people, money and keys. Do not put real data or real keys into the lab.
